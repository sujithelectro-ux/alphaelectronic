2. Information we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, site address and the message or service details you send through the contact form, quote form, chatbot or customer portal.
  • Invoice and quotation details: the items, quantities, prices, payments, notes and warranty information recorded on your invoices and quotations.
  • Account information: your email address, phone number, display name, sign-in method and a Firebase user ID. If you sign in with Google or by SMS code, Google or Firebase verifies you — we never receive your Google password or your SMS code.
  • Browser storage: the website keeps a sign-in session and, in the invoice maker, drafts, item history and price memory in your own browser. This data stays on your device until you clear it.
  • Technical information: our hosting and database provider (Google Firebase) processes standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, timestamps and error logs to deliver and secure the service.

We do not collect card numbers or online card payments on this website, and we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. How we use your information

  • To prepare, issue and explain invoices, quotations and warranty documents.
  • To schedule site visits, installations, maintenance and after-sales support.
  • To reply to your enquiries by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To show you the invoices that Alpha Electronic has assigned to your account.
  • To keep business and tax records as required by law.
  • To protect the service against fraud, abuse and unauthorised access.

4. Your consent

By submitting a form, creating an account or signing in, you agree to this policy. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, but we may still need to keep invoice and warranty records for legal and accounting purposes.

5. How we store and protect it

Accounts, invoices and service messages are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database and Hosting), which acts as our data processor. Access is protected by database security rules, administrator-only permissions and HTTPS. Customers can only read invoices assigned to their own account. Passwords are handled and hashed by Firebase and are never stored by this website.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with:

  • Google Firebase — to run sign-in, storage and website hosting.
  • WhatsApp or your phone network — only when you choose to contact us through them.
  • Authorities — where required by law or to protect legal rights.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage for essential functions: keeping you signed in, remembering invoice drafts and item prices, and storing your local invoice history. These are not advertising cookies. You can clear them from your browser settings, which will sign you out and remove unsent drafts on that device.

8. How long we keep information

Account and invoice records are kept while your account is active and afterwards for as long as needed for warranty, accounting and legal requirements. Service messages are kept until they are no longer needed for support. You can ask us to delete your account details; some invoice records may be retained where the law requires it.

9. Your choices

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you for marketing. Contact us on 077 328 3588 or through WhatsApp. We may ask you to verify your identity before changing account details.

10. Children

This website and portal are intended for adults and business customers. We do not knowingly create accounts for children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The latest version is always available on this page, with the date shown above.

12. Report a problem

If you receive a suspicious message, invoice link or password request that claims to come from Alpha Electronic, or if you believe this website has been misused, please call 077 328 3588 before entering any details. We never ask for your password by phone, WhatsApp or email.
